A **lane** is the sanctioned path for a kind of work. It defines the prerequisites, owners,
required evidence and exits that apply. It is not a synonym for a command name or a row of
checkboxes. A command can enter the lane, but much of the work is routed automatically through
skills and bounded agent roles.

A **gate** is a condition for leaving a phase. It prevents later work from assuming something
that has not been established. A specification approval establishes agreed behavior; a failing
regression establishes a defect that the correction must address; fresh verification establishes
what the current implementation does. None substitutes for the others.

## Choose a lane from the work, not its size

**New observable behavior belongs to feature.** An approved specification or qualifying mini-spec
establishes the behavioral obligations before implementation. The full path includes planning and
attended execution; a small lane trims the artifact ceremony only when all of its classification
conditions hold.

**A confirmed defect belongs to fix.** The regression must reproduce the reported behavior and
fail for the right reason before the minimum correction. An unknown cause starts with debug:
[Investigate and fix](/guides/investigate-and-fix/) explains the evidence and named investigation exits.

**A behavior-preserving change belongs to refactor.** Its primary obligation is parity through
unmodified pre-existing behavioral tests. Changing an expected result to accommodate new behavior
is not evidence of preservation. The [refactor contract](/reference/skills/refactor/) governs that
route rather than the feature diagram below.

**Maintenance uses the relevant maintenance contract.** Documentation, dependency changes and
reverts have different review needs even when grouped under a lighter chore entry. A dependency
change still needs its applicable [dependency review](/guides/adding-a-dependency/); the word
“chore” does not waive supply-chain constraints.

**An exploratory spike ends in findings.** It does not become production work by being on a branch.
The [spike entry](/reference/commands/spike/) owns its disposable scope and non-PR boundary.
Investigation, initialization and read-only review can also end without a PR. “Every change lands
through a PR” does not mean every invocation creates one.

The number of changed lines does not choose the lane. One line can alter a public contract;
hundreds of generated documentation lines can preserve behavior. Start from what must change and
what must remain true.

The scope boundary is particularly important. `executing-plans` delegates one scope to
`subagent-driven-development`. The task engine performs task work and the required combined
review, then returns to that coordinator. It does not jump directly to commit just because one
batch finished. The coordinator owns the next human acknowledgement and final handoff.

## How many gates, concretely

A phase count is less useful than its condition. TDD has six phases: obligation scan, red, green,
obligation verification, coverage and lint. The [test-first concept](/concepts/test-first/) explains
why each exists and why a test run alone is insufficient.

The [commit gate](/reference/skills/commit-gate/) has nine numbered phases covering permission,
branch, classification, verification, behavioral proof, diff review, selective staging, message and
commit. Its conditional provenance auto-heal step is not a separate tenth ordinary phase. A prior
passing result cannot certify a later staged diff merely because both used the same branch name.

The HTML task model also separates task review from whole-scope acceptance. A task displayed as
`REVIEW` is not automatically accepted. The installed engine and applicable host authority must
establish current eligibility and acceptance for the selected identities. Existing Markdown pairs
retain their own supported ledger path; a diagram is not permission to translate between formats.

## STOP, BLOCK and human authority

**STOP surfaces a decision and waits.** A feature checkpoint is an example: the coordinator names
what completed, what comes next and what is open, then waits for the human acknowledgement.

**BLOCK refuses to advance until its condition is satisfied.** A failing regression, incomplete
obligation map or missing current authority cannot become a pass through more confident prose.
Repairing the actual problem and rerunning the check is different from requesting a bypass.

A hard decision boundary can require explicit human authority even during a sprint. Security,
irreversible operations, overrides and merge decisions retain their applicable rules. Other
failures may have a supported correction or recovery path. It is inaccurate to treat every BLOCK
as something only an override can clear, or every user “yes” as enough to clear a mechanical failure.

Where a bypass is permitted, the [override procedure](/guides/overriding-a-gate/) owns its immediate
scope and audit entry. **H-18 activation protection has no in-session override path.** A diagram
must not erase that exception by drawing a universal “continue anyway” arrow.

## Mandatory routing at commit time

Certain changed surfaces bring their own review or marker requirements. Crypto/secret handling
uses its digest-bound marker contract. Migrations have a separate required review marker.
CI and deployment changes route security review, while their post-write reminders are advisory.
These are different enforcement mechanisms, not identical colored boxes.

Use [Hook Gates](/reference/hooks-gates/) to inspect the exact condition and effect. A mandatory
procedure in orchestration prose and a hook that mechanically blocks a tool event have different
proof boundaries. The [enforcement model](/enforcement/) explains the distinction.

## The terminal lane

When the current work clears commit, finishing offers the supported terminal choices. This map
illustrates **Open a PR**. A separate authorized merge still requires the repository’s applicable
exact-head checks. Sprint autonomy ends at the open PR, not at an automatic merge.

Finishing reuses the PR command’s documented procedure in place. It expressly does not call that
entry again. This is why a chart showing an unconditional `/pr` call after every internal skill can
misrepresent the actual route. The [delivery guide](/guides/review-and-ship/) describes the human
handoff and subsequent cleanup.

## Check your understanding

For one real task, identify the behavior being changed or preserved, the lane that owns it, its
first required evidence and the next owner. Then identify the relevant alternate exit: a correction,
a missing decision, a supported resume, or findings without implementation.

Compare those answers with the owning [command and skill references](/reference/). A successful
explanation predicts both the normal path and where that path must stop. Use the
[feature guide](/guides/feature-lane/) to perform the work, rather than treating this explanatory
map as a runnable procedure or an execution receipt.

Neither makes every failed check a user-decision case or authorizes a universal override.
