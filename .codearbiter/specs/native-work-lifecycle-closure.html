Native work lifecycle closure

Make legacy adoption and governed one-item lifecycle commits usable without weakening migration integrity, assistive-state boundaries or delivery truth.

Intent

INTENT-01

Intent

CallerPinned implementation handoff for issues #1084, #1087 and #1089
OutcomeA lossless explicit adoption path and narrow commit-gate lifecycle exemption delivered as one reviewed PR.
ProblemExisting projects cannot complete native adoption for shared origins or long task lines, and legitimate owner-produced lifecycle diffs are rejected as scope creep.

Approach
