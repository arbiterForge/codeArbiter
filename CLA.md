# Contributor agreement status

**No Contributor License Agreement is currently in force.** This page does not
request a signature or create an additional copyright or patent grant. Submitting
a pull request is not acceptance of the earlier draft agreement.

The public project license is [AGPLv3](./LICENSE). Commercial licenses are not
being offered at this time. See [License and contributions](./README.md#license-and-contributions)
for the current public policy.

## Before accepting contributions

Maintainers must not treat a submission as granting proprietary relicensing
rights under this page or the retired draft. Review the contribution's origin
and applicable license. Seek qualified legal advice when additional rights are
needed; do not assume that a future agreement applies retroactively.

A future contributor agreement requires legal review and a separate, explicit
assent process before maintainers can rely on it. No such process is activated
by this change.

The former unreviewed template is retained in Git history for traceability. It
is not an operative agreement and should not be presented to contributors for
signature. This status notice does not alter LICENSE or determine ownership of
past contributions.
